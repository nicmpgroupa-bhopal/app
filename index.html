Child Safety Standards

Last Updated: 16 November 2026

1. Our Commitment

M-ReshamMitra is designed for adult users and is not intended for children. We are committed to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

2. Age Restrictions

Children are not our target audience. We do not knowingly permit children to use our application.

3. Prohibited Activities

  • Child sexual abuse and exploitation.
  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Grooming or exploitation of minors.
  • Any activity that endangers children.

4. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users can report child safety concerns through the reporting functionality available in our app. Reports are handled according to applicable laws and our safety procedures.

5. Action Against Violations

We take appropriate action against violations, which may include account suspension, content removal and reporting to relevant authorities where legally required.
